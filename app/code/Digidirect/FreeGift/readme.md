Free Gift
=================

[wiki link](https://wiki.digidirect.com/display/LEGO/Free+Gift)

### Description

Surprise store visitors with free gift items and products with discounts.
Draw customers' attention to your gifttions using catchy banners and product labels.
Clearly show customer benefits on the shopping cart page.

### VERSION 1.0.0

  1. Automatically add free gifts to shopping carts
  2. Offer gift products with discounts
  3. Show free items and discounted products in the shopping cart
  4. Use banners and product labels to inform customers about gifttions
  5. Display gift items in a handy popup
